# Design System: Wheeler Cat — Variant F

## Variant thesis

**What if Wheeler's chain identity as a Cat® dealer was visible structural language, not just a logo?**

Wheeler is one of 50+ Caterpillar US dealers. The captured surface signals this twice (logo placement, "Cat®" wordmark in copy, "parts.cat.com" link target) but never as structural language. F promotes Caterpillar's iconic equilateral yellow triangle into eyebrow marks, section-divider rules, and a footer wordmark plate.

**No invention.** The Cat-triangle is Caterpillar corporate identity in public use across every authorized dealer. The Wheeler wordmark is the captured logo. F changes scope and scale, not identity.

## Differentiation vs siblings

- **vs A**: section eyebrow vocabulary (none in A vs ▲-marked in F); footer composition (link grid vs wordmark plate); header treatment (captured chrome vs triangle-marked nav dividers).
- **vs D**: F preserves the captured type scale; D rewrites it. F adds the triangle motif; D does not. F's footer wordmark plate is a primitive D doesn't carry.
